# Paper-results summary

This is a non-predictive sensitivity study. Its normalized encounter settings do not describe a measured doxorubicin encounter.

Across the five baseline initial-state benchmarks, primary-superoxide yield per encounter ranged from 0.140445 to 0.445496.
Across the selected grids, the range was 0.000594884 to 1; these extrema are properties of this grid only.
The worst matrix-exponential versus Dormand-Prince absolute discrepancy was 1.044e-12.
The maximum encounter probability-balance error was 1.283e-13; the maximum downstream radical-equivalent balance error was 1.694e-21 M.
Circuit status: executed. Scope: closed coherent unitary simulator/embedding consistency only.

The computations show how assumed selectivity, mixing, relaxation, and escape interact in the declared equations. They do not establish measured D/Q selectivity, encounter coherence, magnetic control, or biological ROS concentrations.
